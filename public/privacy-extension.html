Cre8 Chrome Extension Policy

Privacy Policy for Cre8 Facebook Sync

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Cre8 Facebook Sync ("the Extension") is a productivity tool created for managers and users of the Cre8 Influencer Marketing Platform (https://cre8.la). The Extension enables seamless 1-click synchronization of Facebook viewer session cookies to monitor video metrics and campaign analytics.

2. Data We Access and Handle

The Extension accesses only the minimum required data to perform its single stated functionality:

  • Facebook Session Cookies: Specifically c_user (Facebook User ID) and session tokens (xs, datr, fr, sb).
  • No Passwords: The Extension never sees, handles, or stores your Facebook password.
  • No Messages or Private Content: The Extension does NOT access your inbox, personal messages, feed, contacts, or notifications.

3. Data Usage & Sharing

  • Cookies are transmitted directly and securely to the user's authenticated Cre8 platform instance over encrypted HTTPS.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do NOT sell, lease, monetize, or transfer your session data to any third party, broker, or advertiser.
  • No Tracking: The Extension does not track your browsing activity across other websites.

4. Permissions Justification

  • cookies & *://*.facebook.com/*: Required to read the user's active session cookies on Facebook so they do not need to manually inspect Developer Tools.
  • storage: Stores local settings and user connection status in browser storage.
  • https://*.cre8.la/*: Allows the Extension to safely communicate with the Cre8 Web Application.

5. User Control & Revocation

You may disconnect the session at any time from the Cre8 Web Application (Platforms > Facebook Viewer > Disconnect), or by logging out of Facebook in your browser. You can uninstall the Extension anytime from chrome://extensions.
